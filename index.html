Case study 01 · Semester project

Content Strategy & Website Audit

UNT Technical Communication Department

Problem

The department's website wasn't clearly serving the people it was built for. I spent the semester diagnosing why — and proposing fixes grounded in research rather than opinion.

Process

  1. 1Interviewed stakeholders to understand institutional goals and constraints
  2. 2Ran a full content audit across every existing page
  3. 3Compared Google Analytics behaviour against the site's assumptions about visitors
  4. 4Synthesized findings into four prioritized revision recommendations
  5. 5Built sample wireframes for two of them to make the changes concrete

Outcome

Four ranked recommendations, two of them already drawn up as wireframes — a plan a department could act on incrementally. This was an academic capstone built to develop practical content strategy skills rather than a client engagement, so the recommendations weren’t implemented on the live site; our final deliverable earned an A.

Case study 02 · Internship portfolio

Content & Fundraising Strategy

Tirzah International

Problem

Tirzah International, a nonprofit empowering women globally, needed donor- and grant-facing content plus a fully planned peer-to-peer fundraiser — the kind of work a small team rarely has bandwidth for in-house.

Process

  1. 1Interviewed Emerging Leader Program graduates to surface authentic testimonies
  2. 2Adapted each interview into grant testimonials, donor emails, and global impact stories
  3. 3Wrote and published a full-length blog post
  4. 4Designed a peer-to-peer fundraising event from scratch: invitations, schedule, host manual
  5. 5Presented the finished project to three senior Tirzah staff for evaluation

Outcome

A ready-to-use content and fundraising kit: published writing, reusable donor and grant language drawn from real voices, and an event a volunteer host could run without me in the room. The three senior staff members I presented to responded positively, and were especially interested in adapting the concept into a virtual conference for future use. The materials weren’t used for an event that year, but the underlying idea carried forward as a direction Tirzah wanted to explore.
